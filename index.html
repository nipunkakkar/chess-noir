WELCOME TO THE TABLE

Your move.

Select a piece, then a highlighted square. You can also drag a piece. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move around the board and Enter or Space to select.

  • Protect your king. You cannot make a move that leaves your king in check. Checkmate wins the game.
  • The special moves are here. Castle by moving your king two squares. En passant and all four promotion choices are supported.
  • Play together. Share one device, or create a private room and send your friend the invitation link. The host plays White. Keep the host’s tab open; a disconnect pauses the game.
  • Make it your game. Choose a clock when starting. The clock begins after White’s first move. Takebacks are available on the same device. A draw needs both players’ agreement.
  • Draws. Stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition and the fifty-move rule end the game automatically.

No sign-up. No downloads. Just you, a friend, and 64 squares.
